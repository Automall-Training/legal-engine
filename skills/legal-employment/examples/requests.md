# Example requests

Invented firms only. Each line: the request → what the module does.

- "Нужно принять дизайнера в Example Studio SRL (Кишинёв), на полставки, удалённо" → hiring: part time and
  telework levers, the contract with the Moldovan list, safety training, steps in order; the hiring order offered
  as optional.
- "Можно оформить программиста как ИП, а не по трудовому?" → contractor or employee: the facts weighed, verdict,
  options with cost and risk (a real independent service, part-time employment, an agency); one recommendation.
- "Повысить зарплату и сменить должность сотруднику" → amendment: an addendum with only the changed clauses,
  registration where required.
- "Хотим расстаться с сотрудником, он не справляется" → ending: an agreement first, the performance ground with its
  procedure as the alternative, protected periods checked, payments listed.
- "Angajăm un manager în România pentru firma din Moldova" → cross-border: law of the place of work, registration
  and social security, options (local employer, employer of record, independent service) with cost and risk.
- "Пришла проверка инспекции труда" → inspection: the term first, documents to show, gaps that can be lawfully
  closed now.
