# Law map: employment

Navigation, not law. Every act below is where to **look**; before it carries a conclusion it is read in the
official text in the version in force on the task date (`law-check.md`, `version-check.md`). Article numbers
marked *reported* come from an earlier reading and are the first thing to confirm: both labour codes are amended
several times a year. Amounts (minimum wage, contribution rates, insured income) are set yearly by separate acts
and are never given from memory.

## Cross-border

- **A Moldovan firm, a person working from Romania** (or the reverse): the law of the place of habitual work
  protects the person; Romanian rules on registration, tax and social security may apply to the employer, or the
  engagement may need another structure (an employer of record, a branch, a contractor). Social security between
  Moldova and Romania: the bilateral agreement in force (*reported*); never assumed.
- **Hiring a foreigner**: the right to work in the country (a permit or an exemption) is checked before the start;
  an EU citizen works in Romania freely, a Moldovan citizen with Romanian citizenship as an EU citizen.
- **Posting**: a worker sent to another EU country for a period keeps the home social security only with the
  form of the competent institution.

## Known traps

- **Registration before work.** Romania: the contract goes into REVISAL before the person starts (*reported*:
  at the latest the day before); undeclared work carries heavy fines per person. Moldova: the contract is written
  and signed before work starts; the registration duties of the social insurance law apply.
- **Protected periods.** Dismissal is forbidden or limited during sick leave, pregnancy and maternity, some
  leaves and union mandates in both codes: read the list before any dismissal.
- **Disciplinary dismissal** needs the procedure of the code (a written investigation, the term counted from when
  the employer learnt of the act); a missing step voids the dismissal regardless of the fault.
- **Notice depends on the country, the role and who ends the contract**: read it each time, never copy it from
  another case.
- **Non-compete in Romania** binds only with a monthly indemnity of the code during the restriction (*reported*);
  a non-compete without it is a clause that costs the user credibility and gives nothing.
- **Works made by employees.** Economic rights in works created in the job follow the copyright law of each
  country (RO Law 8/1996, MD Law 230/2022, *reported*); moral rights cannot be waived — draft a use consent, not a
  waiver.
- **Amounts change yearly**: minimum wage, contributions, insured income (IT park) — read on the task date.
