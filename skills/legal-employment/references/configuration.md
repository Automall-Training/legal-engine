# Configuration: how to engage, change and end work at the lowest cost and risk

The question is how the business gets the work done lawfully with the least paperwork and cost at an acceptable
risk (`<legal-workflow>/references/norms.md` §15). Amounts (taxes, contributions, minimum wage) come from the acts
in force on the task date; this file holds the levers, not numbers.

## Ways to engage a person

| Option | Fits when | What it needs | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employment contract, indefinite, full time | regular work under the firm's direction | written contract before the start, registration, safety induction, payroll | cost of payroll; ending needs a ground or an agreement |
| Part time | the work needs fewer hours | the same, hours fixed in the contract | hours worked beyond the contract are overtime; pay at least pro rata |
| Fixed term | a temporary need the code lists (replacement, seasonal, project, other listed cases) | the case named in the contract; limits on renewals | a case not in the list turns it into an indefinite contract |
| Remote or telework | the person can work away from the office | the telework clauses of the code or law; safety rules adapted | the place of work decides which law applies if it is in another country |
| Civil contract with a self-employed person (RO: PFA or similar; MD: an individual entrepreneur or another lawful form) | an independent result or service: own schedule, own tools, several clients, the person bears the risk | a service contract (`legal-contracts`), invoices | requalification as employment, with back taxes and fines, when the work looks like a job |
| A firm or agency that supplies the person (temporary work agency, employer of record, outsourcing) | a short need, another country, no wish to run payroll | the provider's contract; check the provider is authorised where the law requires it | the provider's compliance becomes the user's problem if it is a disguise |
| Day labourer (RO only) | occasional unskilled work in the activities of Law 52/2011 | the register of the act | outside the listed activities it is undeclared work |

## Levers inside a contract

- **Probation** within the legal maximum instead of a fixed term "to test" the person.
- **Pay structure**: a base salary at least the minimum wage, variable parts with clear criteria.
- **Working time**: an individual schedule or flexible hours the code allows, instead of overtime.
- **Place of work**: office, remote or mixed; a mobile workplace clause where the code allows it.
- **Confidentiality** in the contract instead of a separate NDA; **non-compete** only when the protected
  interest is worth the indemnity (Romania) or the rule of the code (Moldova).
- **Sector regimes**: an IT park residency (Moldova) or a sector tax facility (Romania) changes taxes, not the
  labour code; check eligibility on the task date.

## Ending: from the cheapest lawful route

| Route | Needs | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement of the parties | a written agreement with the date and any payment agreed | lowest; the person cannot later claim an unlawful dismissal on a valid agreement |
| Resignation | the person's written notice; the notice period may be waived by the employer | none for the employer when it is freely given |
| End of a fixed term | the term in the contract and the code's conditions | a term that was not lawful makes the contract indefinite |
| Dismissal for a reason not related to the person (job abolished) | a real and serious abolition, notice, consultation where required, payments of the code | a fake abolition is annulled with back pay |
| Dismissal for a reason related to the person (performance, fitness, discipline) | the procedure of the code for that ground, evidence, the terms | a missing step voids it whatever the facts |

A dismissal that the facts do not support is not presented as an option: the table offers the agreement or a
lawful ground instead.

## Method

1. The facts that decide the choice (procedure 5 for contractor or employee).
2. Two to four rows from the tables above that fit: what each needs, the one-off and recurring cost (dated),
   residual risk with its weight, verdict.
3. One recommendation and the trigger that would make another row better (growth, a second country, an inspection).
