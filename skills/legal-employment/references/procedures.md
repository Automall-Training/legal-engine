# Procedures

Each procedure is a checklist with an outcome. The outcome is one or more of: **D** — a document in `output/`
(through [documents.md](documents.md)); **M** — a measure the user takes (who, what, by when); **Q** — one question
to the user, options with the recommended one first. Working notes go to `<matter>/work/` and name the role, not
the person. Article references follow [law-map.md](law-map.md) and are read on the task date before they carry a
conclusion. Each procedure looks for the lawful route with the least cost first ([configuration.md](configuration.md)).

## 1. Hiring

- [ ] Employer, role, start date, hours, pay, place (office, remote, another country → procedure 7), fixed term
      and its case, probation.
- [ ] Contractor or employee? When the user asks for a civil contract or the facts are mixed → procedure 5.
- [ ] Right to work for a foreigner, checked before the start.
- [ ] Documents the law requires for this employer: the contract with the required content (documents.md), the
      information to give before signing (Romania), the safety induction and its record, the employee privacy
      notice (`legal-privacy`). Optional ones (an application, a hiring order where the code makes it optional, a
      job description where not required) are offered as optional.
- [ ] Registration before work starts and its deadline (Romania: REVISAL; Moldova: the duties of the social
      insurance law); payroll set up by the user's accountant.

**Outcome:** D (contract, required annexes, a checklist of the steps in order) · M (sign before the start,
register, induct) · Q (fixed or indefinite, probation length, when unclear).

## 2. Amendment

- [ ] What changes: salary, position, hours, place, remote work, other terms. The current contract and its last
      addenda as the base.
- [ ] By agreement in writing, as a rule. The cases where the employer may change something alone (a temporary
      transfer, a change of place within the code's limits) are read in the code before they are used.
- [ ] Changes that must be registered (Romania: REVISAL deadline for the change; Moldova: as the law requires).
- [ ] A change the person refuses: what the code allows next — keep the contract, agree another change, or a
      lawful ground for ending; never a pressured "agreement".

**Outcome:** D (an addendum with only the changed clauses, or real tracked changes and a clean copy) · M (sign,
register) · Q (when the change needs the person's consent the user does not have yet).

## 3. Ending

- [ ] Who ends it and why: agreement, resignation, end of term, a reason related to the person, a reason not
      related to the person, by law.
- [ ] The cheapest lawful route first (configuration.md, "Ending"): an agreement is offered before a dismissal
      when the facts for a ground are weak.
- [ ] For a dismissal: the ground in the code, the protected periods (sick leave, pregnancy and maternity,
      leaves, union mandates), the procedure (investigation, evaluation, consultation, offer of another vacancy
      where required), notice, the terms counted from the facts.
- [ ] Final pay: salary to date, unused leave compensation, severance where the code gives it, documents to hand
      over (work record, certificates). Amounts from the act in force.
- [ ] Registration of the ending (Romania: REVISAL by its deadline) and the return of company property.

**Outcome:** D (agreement, the decision or order of dismissal with its ground, the notice, a list of payments and
documents) · M (hand over, pay, register) · Q (route, when the user's facts allow several).

## 4. Internal rules and policies

- [ ] Which internal documents the law requires for this employer (Romania: internal regulation; Moldova: the
      internal regulation or the rules the code requires; both: safety documents, harassment rules where the
      code requires them) and which are optional.
- [ ] Only what the law requires plus what removes a medium or higher risk (`norms.md` §7, §8).
- [ ] How the employees are informed (signature, an internal channel) as the code says.

**Outcome:** D (the regulation or policy, short) · M (inform the employees).

## 5. Contractor or employee

- [ ] The facts: who sets the schedule and place, whose tools, instructions or results, exclusivity, payment by
      time or by result, who bears the risk, how long, whether the person has other clients, integration in the
      team.
- [ ] Each fact weighed against the employment marks of the law and the inspectorate's practice in that country.
- [ ] Verdict on the norms scale; the risk names the sanction (back contributions, fines, requalification).
- [ ] The cheapest lawful option: change the facts to a real independent service, or an employment contract with
      the flexible levers (part time, remote, flexible hours), or an agency.

**Outcome:** a short note with the verdict and the options · D (a service contract through `legal-contracts`, or
an employment contract) · Q (which option).

## 6. Inspection or an employee's claim

- [ ] Received when, from whom, what is asked, the term. Urgent first.
- [ ] Documents asked for and those that exist; what is missing and whether it can be lawfully completed now
      (never backdated).
- [ ] For a claim: the facts, the rule, the limitation term, the options (settle, correct, defend) with cost.

**Outcome:** D (the list of documents to show, a reply draft) · M (the user files or hands over) · Q (settle or
defend).

## 7. Work across borders

- [ ] Where the person habitually works, where the employer is, citizenship and right to work, social security.
- [ ] Which law protects the person, which country taxes the salary, which social security applies.
- [ ] Options: an employment contract under the law of the place of work with local registration · an employer of
      record or agency there · a real independent service · a branch. Cost and risk of each.

**Outcome:** a short note with the options table · Q (which option) · M (registrations the user or their
accountant makes).
