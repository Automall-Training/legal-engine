# Law map: personal data

Navigation, not law. Every article below is where to **look**; before it carries a conclusion it is read in the
official text in the version in force on the task date (`law-check.md`, `version-check.md`). Article numbers and
dates marked *reported* come from an earlier reading and are the first thing to confirm.

## Known traps

- The old Moldovan Law 133/2011: never in a new text; consents given under it stay valid if they meet L195/2024
  (*reported*: art. 90(8)). Acts of the Center issued under the old law may still apply (*reported*: art. 90(5));
  which ones is not verified.
- A summary or a secondary site with other article numbers than the official PDF: trust only the official text.
- An EDPB guideline, a CNIL example or a model is guidance: it shapes a recommendation, not a legal requirement.
