# Terms of sale: structure by product

Structure, not law. Each line exists only when [checkpoints.md](checkpoints.md) or a recorded user decision
needs it (`norms.md` §8, clause test); everything else is left out or labelled `recomandare`. By the "where"
rule (`norms.md` §9) much of it lives next to the order button or in the confirmation e-mail rather than in the
terms. One document can cover several products: the common core once, then one block per product type sold.

## Common core (every sale to consumers)

1. Seller identity and contact (C1); version and date of the terms.
2. How the contract is made: steps, correcting errors, the order button, the confirmation (C3, C4).
3. Price and payment: total price, taxes, extra costs, payment means (C2, C9).
4. Withdrawal: whether it exists for this product, the term, how to exercise it, refunds, the official form as a
   separate page (C5, C6) — or the reason it does not apply.
5. Conformity: the legal guarantee and the remedies; a commercial guarantee only if the user gives one (C8).
6. Complaints and out-of-court resolution (C12); law and court without taking away mandatory protection (route
   step 2).
7. Personal data: one link to the notice, drafted by `legal-privacy` (C14).

## Blocks by product

| Product | Block adds | Checkpoints |
|---|---|---|
| Goods (a shop, own delivery or carrier) | delivery term and areas, late delivery, passing of risk, return cost on withdrawal, condition of returned goods, the goods exceptions that really apply | C5, C6, C15 |
| Goods with digital elements (a device with an app or updates) | which digital part is included, update period, compatibility | C8, C15 |
| Service (repairs, consulting, cleaning, a salon booking) | when it starts, the express request to start in the withdrawal period and the proportionate payment, cancellation and no-show rules that pass the unfair-terms test | C5, C11 |
| Digital content or service (download, app, software, online access) | start of access, the consent and acknowledgement before access, technical requirements, updates, changes to the content | C2, C6, C8, C16 |
| Subscription | duration, renewal, trial and the price after it, how to end it, price and content changes | C16 |
| Course, event or booking on a date | schedule, the leisure-on-a-date question, the provider's cancellation, recordings and access after the date; model card M03 | C6, C11 |
| Bundle (goods + service + access) | which rule applies to which part; the bundle is not called "digital content" only because it uses a platform | C5, C6 |
| Sales on a marketplace | which terms the marketplace imposes, what the seller adds, who answers for delivery and returns | C17 |

## Lawful levers to consider (route step 4)

These change duties without breaching them; each is weighed with its cost and risk before it is suggested:

- digital access at once with the consent and acknowledgement (C6), instead of waiting out the withdrawal period;
- one clear return policy for all buyers, set at the legal minimum unless a longer one sells better;
- a marketplace that provides the withdrawal function and the complaint flow (C7, C17);
- selling the professional version only to firms (`legal-contracts`) when the buyers really are firms;
- a short durable-medium confirmation instead of long declarations on the site.
