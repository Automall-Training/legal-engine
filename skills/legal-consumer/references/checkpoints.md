# Checkpoints of a sale to consumers

Go through every point that fits the sale. Each point gets: **met** · **missing** · **not applicable** · **not
verified**, the rule found on the task date (per jurisdiction, see [law-map.md](law-map.md)), the sanction if one
exists, and the simplest lawful fix. The points are what to check, not the text of the law: the article numbers
here are *reported* and are confirmed before they are cited.

| # | Point | What to look at | Romania / EU (where to look) |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Seller identity | legal name, address, registration, contact by e-mail and phone, on the site and in the confirmation | OUG 34/2014 art. 6; Law 365/2002 |
| C2 | Information before the order | main features, total price with taxes and delivery, payment and delivery terms, duration and how to end a subscription, the withdrawal right or its absence, the guarantee of conformity, the complaints policy, digital functionality and compatibility | OUG 34/2014 art. 6 in the version of the task date |
| C3 | Order button | the last step is labelled clearly as an order with an obligation to pay | OUG 34/2014 art. 8(2) |
| C4 | Confirmation on a durable medium | the contract, the information and, for digital content, the consent and acknowledgement below, sent by e-mail after the order | OUG 34/2014 art. 8 |
| C5 | Withdrawal | 14 days, from receipt of goods or from the contract for services and digital content; the official form offered; refund within the legal term; a period extended when the information was missing; a service started in the withdrawal period at the consumer's express request → on withdrawal the consumer pays a proportionate amount for what was performed (Dir. 2011/83 art. 14(3)) | OUG 34/2014 withdrawal articles and annex |
| C6 | Exceptions to withdrawal | the list of the act is closed — use only what fits: goods made to the consumer's specifications or personalised, perishable goods, sealed goods unsealed for health or hygiene reasons, goods mixed inseparably, sealed recordings or software unsealed; digital content without a medium: lost only with the consumer's prior express consent, the acknowledgement that the right is lost, and the confirmation (C4); services fully performed after the same consent; accommodation, transport of goods, car rental, catering or leisure on a specific date (a course or event on a fixed date may be leisure — check, do not assume) | OUG 34/2014 exceptions article; Dir. 2011/83 art. 16 |
| C7 | Online withdrawal function | from 19.06.2026 (*reported*): a function on the same interface, labelled for withdrawal, a confirmation step, an acknowledgement on a durable medium with content, date and time; the platform provides it, the seller makes sure it is there | OUG 34/2014 as amended by OUG 18/2026; Dir. 2011/83 art. 11a |
| C8 | Conformity guarantee | goods: legal guarantee of conformity; digital content: conformity, updates, remedies; a commercial guarantee only adds | OUG 140/2021; OUG 141/2021 |
| C9 | Prices and discounts | total price shown; a reduction announced against the lowest price of the previous 30 days (with the exceptions of the act: perishable goods, new products, progressive reductions); personalised prices disclosed | reductions: OG 99/2000 and HG 947/2000 as amended (*reported*), Dir. 98/6 art. 6a; personalised prices: OUG 34/2014 art. 6 |
| C10 | Claims and reviews | no misleading claims; "free" only when free; how reviews are checked, if shown; no fake urgency | Law 363/2007 |
| C11 | Unfair terms | no term that removes legal rights, lets the seller change the price or content at will, or keeps the whole paid amount on any withdrawal (the proportionate amount of C5 is the lawful route) | Law 193/2000 |
| C12 | Complaints | a complaints channel and the complaints policy; information on out-of-court resolution (SAL) and the ANPC information on the site where required; no outdated ODR link | OUG 34/2014 as amended; OG 38/2015 and the ANPC order on site information (*reported*); Reg. 2024/3228 |
| C13 | Language | the language the buyer's country requires for consumer information | OG 21/1992 and the act in force |
| C14 | Personal data | a privacy notice and cookie consent exist — handed to `legal-privacy` when on | — |
| C15 | Delivery and risk (goods) | delivery term stated (by default within the term of the act), what happens on a late delivery, risk passes when the consumer or their chosen carrier receives the goods, who pays the return cost on withdrawal (the consumer only when told before the order) | OUG 34/2014 delivery and risk articles; Dir. 2011/83 art. 14(1), 18, 20 |
| C16 | Subscriptions and automatic renewal | the duration, the renewal, the price after a trial or an introductory period and how to end it are shown before the order and confirmed; ending is not harder than signing up; a price or content change follows a term the act allows | OUG 34/2014 art. 6; Law 193/2000; OUG 141/2021 (changes to digital content) |
| C17 | Marketplace and platform | on a marketplace: the buyer is told whether the seller is a trader (consumer rights apply) and who is responsible for what; the platform's own duties (ranking, reviews) belong to the platform | OUG 34/2014 as amended by the 2019/2161 transposition; Dir. 2011/83 art. 6a |
| C18 | Not a distance sale | in the shop, at an event or at the consumer's home: off-premises sales have their own withdrawal and form duties; a sale in the shop has the general information duties only, no legal withdrawal (a voluntary return policy is `recomandare`) | OUG 34/2014 (off-premises and art. 5); OG 21/1992 |

## Moldova

For a sale to consumers in Moldova, the same points are checked against Law 105/2003, the Civil Code articles on
consumer and distance contracts and the e-commerce law in force (see the law map). The withdrawal period and its
exceptions, the information list, the guarantee terms and the sanctions are found for each matter; when a point
cannot be found on the task date it is **not verified** with a working conclusion and its risk, never filled
from the Romanian column.

## How to report

One table: point · status · rule (act, article, version date) · sanction · fix · `cerință legală` or
`recomandare`. Then the changes for the terms (tracked changes or a new draft) and a short list for the web
developer (button label, tick box, confirmation e-mail, withdrawal function). The user decides and makes the
changes.
