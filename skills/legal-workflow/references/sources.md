## Weight of a source

- **Normative act:** check the issuing body, its rank, entry into force, scope and the applicable version.
- **Court decision:** establish separately whether it binds and how far; not every decision is a general precedent.
- **Regulator's decision:** normative or individual, addressees, status.
- **Guidelines, recommendations, FAQ, press releases:** official reading or information, not law by default.
- **Model or form:** call it mandatory or officially approved only after checking the approving act and its scope.
- **Professional practice:** label it a contractual recommendation. It never proves a rule in force, but it is an
  argument about enforcement risk (how the market works, whether the authority actually sanctions).

When sources disagree, check version, date, language, which rule is special, publication and hierarchy. Never pick
the answer most sites give. Keep an unresolved conflict and its effect in the register.
