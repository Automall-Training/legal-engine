# Which law and which forum

Matrix: question → relevant links → candidate regime → verified basis → conclusion or unknown. Different questions
of one matter may fall under different regimes. Governing law, choice of law, mandatory rules, forum and the
data-protection regime are separate decisions.

## Method

1. Establish the parties' establishments and actual activity, habitual residence, whether a natural person acts as
   a consumer, the place of performance or of the property, where the offer is directed and the possible forum.
2. Check any choice of law: validity, scope, exclusions. Without a clause, research the conflict rules of the
   relevant forum. The language or one party's registration does not decide it.
3. Research separately the competent court, jurisdiction, arbitrability, seat and rules of arbitration, and
   recognition and enforcement. A choice of law does not appoint a court.
4. Check overriding mandatory rules, public policy, consumer protection, form and international treaties as the
   facts require.
5. For each processing of personal data establish the national regime and whether the GDPR applies, regardless of
   the contractual choice of law.

When a decisive fact is unknown, give conditional options with their basis and name the fact that selects one.
Financial services, employment, real estate, IP, competition, public procurement and other special areas need
their own research; the general checklist is not enough by itself.

## Moldova

Start with [legis.md](https://www.legis.md/): full text, history, published amendments, the applicable version. For
a commercial matter research the Civil Code (`Codul civil`) and the special rules on the subject, form, conflict of
laws, procedure and arbitration. Match old articles by content and official amendments, never by number alone.

Distinguish `rezoluțiune`, `reziliere`, `denunțare`, `încetare`, `penalitate`, `daune-interese` in the applicable
version. Do not carry over the numbering or mechanisms of the Romanian code.

For personal data check the national law and its transitional rules on the date. The supervisory authority
(CNPDCP) announced the move from Law no. 133/2011 to Law no. 195/2024 from 23.08.2026; this is a navigation signal,
not a check of any article or later amendment — open the full text and its history every time.

A national law close to the GDPR does not make the EU regulation itself apply. Adequacy for transfers from the EU
is checked against the Commission's decision in force, never assumed.

Form, registration, notarisation, electronic signature, authority to sign and a mandatory language are checked for
the concrete document. IDNO, address and bank details come only from confirmed data.

## Romania

Start with the [Portal Legislativ](https://legislatie.just.ro/). Find the Civil Code and the special acts, pick the
version date, check the history and Monitorul Oficial. The date a page was opened does not prove the consolidation
is complete.

For B2B check formation, authority, form, subject, determinable price, standard terms, special requirements for
particular clauses, performance and termination. For B2C add the mandatory consumer, distance-contract and digital
content rules as the facts require.

Keep substantive law, jurisdiction, arbitration and enforcement apart. With international links research the scope
of EU instruments and treaties; a Romanian party alone does not settle it.

For personal data research the GDPR and the national additions. Navigation candidates: Law no. 190/2018, Law
no. 506/2004 and ANSPDCP decisions; this list confirms neither version nor applicability. Cookies and marketing
have their own rules, separate from GDPR Article 6.

Use the terminology of the applicable law; do not mix different termination mechanisms. CUI/CIF, registration
number and representative are never filled in by guess.

## Cross-border

Map the contractual links, the possible forums, each participant's processing operations and the international
transfers.

**Contract.** Identify the forum and its conflict rules. For an EU forum research the material, temporal and
territorial scope of Rome I (593/2008), where needed Rome II (864/2007), and for jurisdiction and enforcement
Brussels I bis (1215/2012). These are candidates to research, not rules that apply automatically. For a Moldovan
forum research the national rules and international treaties.

For an international sale check the CISG separately: the states' participation on the date, declarations, scope,
grounds of application and exclusion by the parties. For arbitration: validity of the agreement, seat, institution,
current rules, arbitrability and recognition. Never invent an institution. Check consumer and other protective or
overriding rules whatever law was chosen.

**Data.** Open the applicable Articles 2 and 3 GDPR and the relevant official guidance:

- Establish any establishment in the EU and the link of the processing to its activity.
- For a participant not established in the EU, research offering goods or services to people in the EU or
  monitoring their behaviour there. Citizenship, the site's language, its accessibility or one counterparty does
  not finish the test.
- Check controller and processor separately. An Article 28 contract and the direct territorial application of the
  GDPR to a processor are separate questions.
- Analyse a representative, a DPO and special exemptions only through the conditions of the rules concerned.
- For a transfer establish the exporter, the recipient, the countries, whether the persons are independent, and
  remote access. The GDPR applying to the recipient does not replace the Chapter V check.

Re-check adequacy decisions, the permitted mechanism, the SCC module, the transfer assessment and supplementary
measures. Article 28 and consent are not universal transfer mechanisms. Official SCCs are changed only within the
limits they allow.

With missing facts, name them and give conditional options. Forbidden shortcuts: "a Moldovan company, so the GDPR
does not apply" and "a client in the EU, so everything is under the GDPR".
