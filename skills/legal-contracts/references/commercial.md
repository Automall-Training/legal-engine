# Commercial documents

Pick the checklist by subject and facts. Each legal requirement is confirmed again in the law check; the items below
are questions to research, not a claim that every deal has the same requirements.

| Block | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| Parties | Legal capacity, authority, identification, consumer status |
| Subject | Determinable result, goods or rights; scope of work, exclusions, specification |
| Performance | Terms, dependencies, acceptance and objections, evidence, change of the assignment |
| Payment | Price or formula, currency, taxes where needed, invoice, stages, refunds; recompute the arithmetic |
| Risk | Quality, warranties, defect cure, risk of accidental loss for the subject |
| Liability | Grounds, causation, penalties and interest, how remedies combine, whether limits are allowed |
| Termination | Grounds, notice, cure period, settlement, return of property and data, surviving terms |
| Dispute | Law, forum, arbitration, language and seat, service, enforceability of the award or judgment |
| Structure | Definitions, numbering, references, annexes, dates, party details, signatures and form |

Kinds of deal:

- **Services:** measurable results, the client's access and materials, acceptance criteria, subcontracting, IP in the
  result, fixes, change requests. Never declare tacit acceptance lawful without context and a check.
- **Supply:** description and quantity, delivery and acceptance, passing of risk and title, defects, documents,
  returns. If Incoterms are used, check the chosen edition, the exact term and the named place; never insert them by
  default.
- **Licence:** right holder, object and version, rights and limits, territory, term, users, exclusivity, sublicence,
  source code and updates, end of access. A licence and an assignment of rights are different.
- **NDA:** definition, exclusions, permitted persons and purposes, compelled disclosure, duration, return or deletion
  and permitted archives. Confidentiality never makes a processing of personal data lawful.
- **Amendment:** exact identification of the base contract, the changed clauses, the effective date; past effects
  only on instruction and after a legal check; annexes and precedence; the historical signature is never changed.
- **Other deals:** add a subject module from verified sources; this list is not closed.

Never insert a country, arbitration, penalty, disclaimer or IP assignment as "standard". Note the benefit and the
risk for the represented party; balance does not mean widening its rights at will. The user's preferred clauses
from `context/house-style.md` are proposals, used only where the checked law allows them for this deal.

A clause on personal data inside a commercial contract is drafted here; a privacy policy, cookie notice, processing
agreement (DPA), transfer clauses or a record of processing is the `privacy` module's deliverable (see `SKILL.md`).
