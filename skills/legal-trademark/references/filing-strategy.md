# Filing strategy: the cheapest protection that covers the business

The question is not "can this be registered?" but "which filing protects what the business really sells, where
it really sells, at the lowest cost and risk" (`<legal-workflow>/references/norms.md` §15). Fees, terms and
fund rules are read on the task date from the office's current list; this file holds the levers, not amounts.

## Levers

| Lever | Choices | Cost and risk effect |
|---|---|---|
| Territory | MD (AGEPI) · RO (OSIM) · EU (EUIPO, covers RO) · Madrid through the home office | protect where the business sells now and plans within the term; each extra territory adds fees and conflicts |
| Route | national filings · one EU filing · Madrid designating MD, the EU and others | Madrid is cheaper for several countries but depends on the base mark for five years; EU is one right that falls as a whole |
| Order and priority | file first where it is cheapest or most urgent, extend abroad within six months with priority | spreads the cost; priority keeps the first date for the later filings |
| Sign | word mark · figurative or combined · both later | a word mark protects the name in any form and is usually the most useful one; a logo protects the get-up; a weak word may pass only with its logo (protection then narrower) |
| Classes and terms | only the goods and services used or really planned; precise terms from the classification database | fewer classes cost less and meet fewer conflicts; too broad invites bad-faith and non-use attacks |
| Owner | the firm that uses the mark · a person who licenses it to the firms | one owner simplifies enforcement; a person as owner needs a licence to each firm that uses it (`<legal-contracts>` model card M05) |
| Representation | own filing · a registered representative | own filing where the law allows it (domicile or establishment in the EEA for EUIPO and OSIM, *reported*); a representative is a cost the law requires only in the cases named in the law map |
| Support | the current SME Fund edition or national support | may refund part of the fees; check eligibility and dates on the task date |

## Method

1. **Facts**: what is sold and under which sign, where buyers are now and in the next years, which firm sells,
   budget, any earlier use or filing, any conflict already known.
2. **Clearance first** (procedure 1) for the signs and territories in play: a filing that meets an earlier right
   costs the fee and maybe an opposition.
3. **Two to four configurations** in one table: territory · route · sign · classes · owner · what it costs now and
   at renewal (from the current fee lists, with the date) · residual risk with its weight · verdict. The first row
   is the cheapest one that covers today's market.
4. **One recommendation**, with the trigger that would justify the next step (a new country, a marketplace that
   requires a registration for brand protection, an investor).
5. **What happens until registration**: the name may be used; an application gives a filing date and, after
   publication, the limited rights of the law map; `®` only after registration.

## Typical answers (check each against the facts)

- A Moldovan business selling only in Moldova: national AGEPI filing, word mark, the classes used.
- A business selling in Moldova and in several EU countries: one national filing first, then within six months
  an EU filing or a Madrid designation of the EU, whichever the current fees and the representation rules make
  cheaper.
- Selling only in Romania: OSIM is usually cheaper than EUIPO; EUIPO when other EU countries follow.
- A marketplace brand programme that asks for a registration: some accept a pending application — read the
  programme's rules on the task date before filing in a hurry.
